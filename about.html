WHO WE ARE
------------------

About Ruhuna Events

Ruhuna Events is a student-focused platform designed to bring University events together in one place. It helps students discover upcoming events, explore activities from different faculties and stay connected with university life.


From academic programmes to cultural and sports events, We make it easier for students to find opportunities and participate in university events.



OUR PURPOSE
------------------

What Drive Us?

🎯Our Mission

To provide a simple and accessible platform where students can discover university events,
stay informed and actively participate in academic, cultural, sports and social activities.

🎖️Our Vision

To create a connected and active university community where every student has the opportunity
to explore experiences, build connections and make meaningful memories.
